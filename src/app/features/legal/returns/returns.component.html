Returns & Refunds

Last updated: 1 May 2025

Under Spanish and EU consumer law you have 14 calendar days from delivery to return most items for any reason. We go further — we offer 30 days for a full refund.

1. Your Right to Withdraw

In accordance with Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007 (General Law for the Defence of Consumers and Users) and EU Directive 2011/83/EU, you may withdraw from your purchase within 14 calendar days of receiving your order without giving any reason. {{ co.name }} voluntarily extends this period to 30 calendar days.

2. Eligible Items

Items are eligible for return if they are:

  • Unopened and in their original sealed packaging
  • In the same condition as received
  • Not a perishable or hygiene-sensitive product that has been opened

For health and safety reasons, we cannot accept returns of opened food supplements or protein products.

3. How to Request a Return

Email {{ co.email.returns }} with your order number and reason for return. We will reply within 2 business days with a return authorisation and shipping instructions.

You are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item is faulty or was sent in error. We recommend using a tracked service.

4. Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return we will process a full refund to your original payment method within 5–7 business days. You will receive an email confirmation when the refund is issued.

Original delivery charges are refunded only if the entire order is returned due to our error or a faulty product.

5. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged or you receive the wrong item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery at {{ co.email.general }} and include a photo. We will arrange a replacement or full refund at no cost to you.

6. Contact

For any return or refund query contact our customer service team at {{ co.email.general }} {{ co.supportHours }}.
