Shipping Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2025

We deliver to all peninsular Spain, the Balearic Islands, and the Canary Islands. Orders placed before 14:00 (CET) on a business day are dispatched the same day.

1. Delivery Areas

We currently ship to:

  • Peninsular Spain — standard and express options available
  • Balearic Islands — standard shipping only
  • Canary Islands — standard shipping only; subject to local customs duties
  • Ceuta and Melilla — standard shipping; customs may apply

We do not currently ship outside Spain.

2. Delivery Times

Estimated delivery times after dispatch:

  • Peninsular Spain: 1–2 business days (standard), next business day (express)
  • Balearic Islands: 2–4 business days
  • Canary Islands: 4–7 business days

These are estimates and not guaranteed. Delivery times may be extended during public holidays, sales periods, or due to carrier delays beyond our control.

3. Shipping Costs

  • Standard shipping: €3.99
  • Express shipping (peninsular only): €6.99
  • Free standard shipping on orders over €40

4. Tracking

Once your order ships you will receive an email with a tracking number and a link to follow your parcel in real time. Our logistics partner covers all regions of Spain.

5. Failed Delivery

If a delivery attempt is unsuccessful the carrier will leave a notification card with instructions to rearrange delivery or collect from a local drop-off point. Parcels not collected within 7 days will be returned to us and we will contact you to arrange re-delivery (additional shipping charges may apply).
